WELCOME TO VARANASI

A City of Heritage & Culture

Varanasi is one of India's most culturally significant cities. Situated on the banks of the River Ganga, the city is known for its historic ghats, temples, music, literature, traditional crafts, festivals and spiritual traditions. It is one of India's most important cultural and spiritual destinations.

TOURISM IN VARANASI

Experience the World's Spiritual Heritage

Varanasi attracts visitors with its ancient temples, historic ghats, traditional music, festivals, handicrafts, spiritual traditions and local food. A visit provides an opportunity to experience India's rich heritage and culture in one place.

MUST VISIT

Famous Tourist Places

🛕 Kashi Vishwanath Temple

One of the most important Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Shiva and a major pilgrimage destination.

🌊 Dashashwamedh Ghat

A famous ghat known for the spectacular evening Ganga Aarti performed every day.

☸️ Sarnath

A significant Buddhist heritage site where Gautama Buddha is traditionally said to have delivered his first sermon.

🌅 Assi Ghat

A peaceful and popular ghat where visitors enjoy sunrise, boat rides and cultural activities.

One of the oldest and most significant cremation ghats located along the River Ganga.

A historic fort situated near the Ganga, known for its architecture, museum and royal heritage.

PLAN YOUR VISIT

Best Time to Visit Varanasi

🍂 October – November

Pleasant weather and several cultural and religious festivals make this period attractive for visitors.

❄️ December – February

Cool weather is suitable for sightseeing, temple visits and exploring the ghats.

🌸 March – April

A good period to experience festivals, local traditions and the colourful atmosphere of the city.

About Varanasi Heritage Project

The Varanasi Heritage project aims to digitally present the historical, cultural, religious and tourism significance of Varanasi in an easy-to- explore website. The project combines web technologies with digital heritage documentation to make information about the city accessible to students, tourists and visitors.
