THE CULTURAL SOUL

Where Tradition Meets Everyday Life

Varanasi is renowned for its deep cultural traditions and spiritual heritage. Its music, religious practices, festivals, food, handloom traditions and artistic expressions form an important part of the city's identity.

CULTURE & TRADITIONS

Explore Varanasi's Cultural Heritage

Classical Music

Varanasi has a rich musical tradition associated with Hindustani classical music, devotional singing and traditional musical performances.

Spiritual Traditions

Daily rituals, prayers, temple traditions and ceremonies along the Ganga are deeply connected with the cultural identity of Varanasi.

Traditional Arts

The city is associated with traditional arts, crafts, paintings, textiles and artistic practices passed through generations.
